DEADLYBUZZ

DeadlyBuzz

The dubstep and DJ side. Ran his own nights LEXES (The Savoy, Top Floor and The Globe, Limerick), Filthy Dubstep, Sweatbox and Nancy Shnare (ShnareCo), played for Macronite, Evolution DnB, USS (Underground Sound Syndicate), Electric Underground and Bastardo Electrico, helped run The Great Friday Festival and The Night After Christmas, and played LIFE Festival, BUMP and Mystik Festival. By 2012 he had been promoting for about 11 years, and got onto Mutant Bass Records by emailing them “Found My Squgee”. Winner of IDMA Best Up And Coming DJ in 2011.

As DeadlyBuzz he has played alongside Reeko and Strangeways (Surge at The Twisted Pepper, Dublin, 2012) and Phonetik (Filthy Dubstep at Bakers, Limerick).
