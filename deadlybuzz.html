DeadlyBuzz

DeadlyBuzz is his dubstep and DJ name, going back to 2001 when he was resident DJ Deadly Buzz at LEXES. He started Macronite and ran Filthy Dubstep, Sweatbox at Bakers Underground, Filthstep and Nancy’s Shnare. He played for USS, Electric Underground, Ctrl-Alt-Delete and Basswax, and got onto Mutant Bass Records by emailing them “Found My Squgee”. He won IDMA Best Up And Coming DJ in 2011, and Flip It TV interviewed him at Bump Muzik Festival in 2010.

As DeadlyBuzz he has played alongside Reeko and Strangeways (Surge at The Twisted Pepper, Dublin, 2012) and Phonetik (Filthy Dubstep at Bakers, Limerick).
