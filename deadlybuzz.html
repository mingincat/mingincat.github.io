DEADLYBUZZ

DeadlyBuzz

The dubstep and DJ side. Ran the club nights Lexes, Headroom, Sweatbox and Filthstep in Limerick, helped run The Great Friday Festival and The Night After Christmas, and played LIFE Festival, BUMP and Mystik Festival. By 2012 he had been promoting for about 11 years, and got onto Mutant Bass Records by emailing them “Found My Squgee”. Winner of IDMA Best Up And Coming DJ in 2011.

As DeadlyBuzz he has played alongside Reeko and Strangeways (Surge at The Twisted Pepper, Dublin, 2012) and Phonetik (Filthy Dubstep at Bakers, Limerick).
