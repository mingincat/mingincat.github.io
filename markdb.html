Mark DB

Mark DB is his main producer and DJ name, for dark techno-bassline released through Play It by Ear Records. As Mark DB he has played Evolution Drum and Bass nights in Limerick, Cork and West Cork alongside Youngsta, Amit, Distance and Thelem, and played Nancy’s Shnare in Cork.

As Mark DB he has supported Youngsta (Tempa / Rinse) and Distance (Chestplate / Planet Mu / Tectonic) at Dolans, Limerick, Jet Li at Pharmacia, Limerick, and Thelem at The Pav, Cork, playing Evolution nights alongside Warren Knowles, Mike Fate, Romeo:Butcher, Cas:Et and Ricard “Entropy” (Liquid Lounge, Cork).
