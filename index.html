Bio

Mark db is an Irish electronic musician and composer, working the darker edges of electronic music since 2006 — from dubstep and drum & bass to the dark techno-bassline he plays today.

DJ, producer, promoter and remixer. His sets run fast and relentless, shifting from ominous, atmospheric vibes to straight-up dancefloor bangers.

Based in Limerick, he ran his own club nights LEXES, Filthy Dubstep, Sweatbox and Nancy Shnare (ShnareCo), started Macronite in Limerick, and played for crews and promoters including Evolution (Mike Fate, drum and bass), USS (Underground Sound Syndicate), Electric Underground and Bastardo Electrico. He also helped run The Great Friday Festival and The Night After Christmas, and has played LIFE Festival, BUMP and Mystik Festival.

He ran LEXES, the Limerick club night, from 2001 as separate nights at three venues, The Savoy, Top Floor and The Globe (where he played DJ Deadly Buzz vs DJ Raven), playing as resident DJ Deadly Buzz, and came back to DJ its 2017 charity reunion at Dolans Upstairs, with 100% of the door going to charity.

By 2012 he had been promoting for about 11 years. He got onto Mutant Bass Records by emailing them his track “Found My Squgee”.

Winner of IDMA Best Up And Coming DJ in 2011, founder of Play It by Ear Records, and also heard under the monikers DeadlyBuzz and Daigoro.

He has played alongside Clark (Electric Underground), Youngsta and Distance (both at Evolution / Macronite, Limerick), Marcus Intalex, Goth-Trad (Dolans), J:Kenzo, Strangeways (Dublin), Fish Go Deep (Dolans), Kanji Kinetic (Sweatbox, Limerick), Thelem (The Pav, Cork), Stray, Squire of Gothos, Jamie Behan and Dan Sykes, and has also shared bills with Reeko, Phonetik, Jet Li, Warren Knowles, Mike Fate, Romeo:Butcher, Cas:Et and Ricard “Entropy”.
