Bio

Mark DB is a DJ, producer and promoter from Limerick, and he has been running nights since 2001. He was making music before he ever DJed. He started with electro and worked through hip-hop, dubstep, techno and drum & bass, and today he plays dark techno-bassline. Like his big influence, Mike Patton, he won’t stick to one sound. He also goes by DeadlyBuzz and Oedo.

He started LEXES in 2001, with separate nights at The Savoy, Top Floor and The Globe, and came back to DJ its 2017 charity reunion at Dolans. He started Macronite, and ran Filthy Dubstep (taking Filthy Dubstep Bastards to Crawdaddy in Dublin), Sweatbox at Bakers Underground, Filthstep at Dolans Warehouse and Nancy’s Shnare in Cork. He has played for Evolution DnB, USS (Underground Sound Syndicate), Electric Underground, Bastardo Electrico, Ctrl-Alt-Delete and Basswax. He also helped run The Great Friday Festival and The Night After Christmas, and has played LIFE Festival, Bump Muzik Festival and Mystik Festival.

He won IDMA Best Up And Coming DJ in 2011, got onto Mutant Bass Records by emailing them his track “Found My Squgee”, and founded Play It by Ear Records. Along the way he has played alongside Clark, Marcus Intalex, Youngsta, Amit, Distance, Goth-Trad and J:Kenzo.
